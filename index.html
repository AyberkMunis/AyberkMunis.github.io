Who we are

A student team built around AI research.

At MALTO - Machine Learning @ PoliTO, students work together on challenging problems in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence, gaining hands-on research experience along the way.

Our core activity is participating in international ML and AI challenges, often hosted by major scientific conferences. Typically lasting four to six months, these challenges follow the structure of a research project, with a concrete problem, a dataset and a shared evaluation framework.

We explore the literature, reproduce baselines and develop and evaluate our own approaches. We then bring our findings together in a scientific paper, with the opportunity to present our work at a conference if accepted. Experienced members mentor newer teammates throughout the process, helping them build the skills and confidence to contribute.

Beyond research, we organise hands-on workshops, MALTALKS guest talks and a Data Science Hackathon, creating opportunities for students across PoliTO to discover AI, learn new skills and connect with the community.
