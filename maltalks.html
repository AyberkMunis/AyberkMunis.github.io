About the series

Real AI, from the people building it

Launched in February 2026, MALTALKS invites guests from research labs, universities and companies to talk about how Artificial Intelligence is designed, evaluated and used in practice.

Every talk is open to all PoliTO students. It’s a chance to explore research and career paths, see how AI is applied outside the classroom and ask questions directly to people working in the field.
