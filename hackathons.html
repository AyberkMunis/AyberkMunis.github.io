What is a MALTO Hackathon?

Put your Machine Learning skills into practice

MALTO Hackathons invite students to tackle practical Machine Learning problems, experiment with different approaches and develop their own solutions. They offer an opportunity to learn by doing, exchange ideas and experience the challenges of working with real data.

Our hackathons also help us meet potential new members. We value curiosity, clear reasoning and creativity alongside performance, encouraging participants to explain their choices and share what they learned.
