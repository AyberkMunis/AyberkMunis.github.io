Our trainees are students taking their first steps into AI research. Through regular training sessions led by senior members, they build the foundations in Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP and Computer Vision needed to join future challenges.

Trainee /treɪˈniː/ Trainees are MALTO members who follow our training program to prepare for international Machine Learning and AI challenges.
