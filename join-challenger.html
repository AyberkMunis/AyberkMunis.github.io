What we look for

Strong skills, shared ambition

We are looking for students with strong Machine Learning knowledge, both in theory and in practice. People who are ambitious and who also value teamwork, so that we can keep growing MALTO together while chasing new results.

Opportunities
  • Compete in international challenges
  • Develop AI solutions
  • Do research together
  • Write scientific papers
  • Be part of Politecnico di Torino’s AI community
  • A chance to attend international conferences
  • Support our events and grow your network
Recruitment process

From hackathon to team

  1. Step 01Join the hackathon & applyTake part in the hackathon we organise that term and apply through the form, sending your CV and transcript.
  2. Step 02CompeteAim for a strong ranking while building the most creative solution you can.
  3. Step 03ShortlistAfter the hackathon, the best-fitting candidates are selected based on hackathon results and CV screening.
  4. Step 04InterviewOur chance to get to know you better. Knowing the teammates we will walk alongside matters to us.
  5. Step 05Welcome to MALTOYou’re in. A new story begins.
